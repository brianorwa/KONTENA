The mechanics

One container, many small importers

Kontena is a digital version of what freight forwarders call LCL consolidation — "less than container load" — except every backer can see the manifest fill up, pay by M-Pesa, and track it themselves.

01

An agent opens a container

A vetted sourcing agent lists a route, closing date, and 12–20 CBM of capacity.

02

Traders book space

Individuals reserve CBM for their own goods and pay a deposit via M-Pesa into escrow.

03

Threshold reached

If the container hits ~80% by the closing date it proceeds; if not, deposits are refunded or the date extends.

04

Sourcing & QC

The agent buys and inspects each trader's goods against photos/specs before packing.

05

Seal & sail

Container is sealed, bill of lading issued, and it sails Guangzhou/Yiwu → Mombasa (~30–35 days).

06

Customs clearance

KRA duty, VAT, IDF and railway levy are apportioned per trader by CBM and declared value.

07

Nairobi warehouse

Cleared goods are trucked in and sorted by booking reference for collection.

08

Balance & pickup

Traders settle the balance, then collect or arrange last-mile delivery.
